Key takeaways

Attractions drive price. The attractions index has the strongest link to price (0.43), followed by capacity (0.29) and bedrooms (0.26).
Size beats location. A 4-bedroom home averages about €252 versus €115 for 1 bedroom, while distance to the center has a weak effect (-0.07).
Superhosts are not pricier. Superhosts average €120 versus €134 for other hosts, so their edge is satisfaction, not price.
